01 The concept

Not the agency model.
A venture model.

We identify promising ideas, evaluate their potential and partner with selected founders to turn them into market-ready products and businesses. Roxten contributes expertise, team, time, technology and execution. You bring the vision, business understanding, commitment and direction.

Upfront cost No conventional upfront development fees for selected founders.
Relationship A long-term partnership that continues after launch.
Approach Understand the vision, challenge assumptions, shape the product.
Incentive The venture actually succeeding.
Return An agreed share of the venture's future earnings.

07 Questions

Before you apply.

Do I pay development fees upfront?

No conventional upfront development fees for selected founders. Roxten contributes its capabilities and resources in exchange for an agreed share of the venture's future earnings.

Who owns the intellectual property?

IP ownership, revenue-share percentage, responsibilities and duration are all defined in a mutual agreement for each selected venture — before any building starts.

Do I need a company or a technical background?

No. You don't need an existing company, technical skills or a developed product. We look for idea potential and founder commitment.

How do you choose which ideas to build?

We evaluate each idea on potential, feasibility, market opportunity, originality and the founder's commitment.

What happens after I apply?

Our team reviews every application personally. If your idea is shortlisted, we'll reach out to set up a discovery conversation.
